Bios

Kelechi Ibe (Co-Founder/CEO)

Kelechi has been a corporate and tax lawyer for 10 years. He is a Chartered Tax Adviser, Ex-KPMG and Udo Udoma & Belo-Osagie, with a stint at Latham & Watkins (London).

World Tax has ranked him 3x as a Rising Star in General Corporate Tax and Transfer Pricing. He serves as 2nd Vice Chairperson of the Taxation Committee at the Nigerian Bar Association’s Section on Business Law.

The 2022 CEOs Network Africa Awards named him Lawyer of the Year and he was nominated for the Future Awards Africa Prize for Lawyers - a Youth Award lauded by the World Bank and the Forbes

Ayo Samuel

Sam Ayo is a technology leader, AI research engineer, and the Chief Technology Officer at TaxStreem, where he is building an AI-native tax and financial infrastructure platform for emerging markets. His work centers on a defining challenge of modern applied AI: transforming capable models into dependable infrastructure for consequential, real-world regulatory and financial systems. At TaxStreem, he leads the engineering organization to architect systems that seamlessly connect machine intelligence with complex data and financial workflows.

His technical foundation combines computer science, machine learning, and production engineering with a deep background in Mathematics, Statistics, Quantitative Finance, and Economics. This multidisciplinary expertise provides him with an unusually broad view of both the mathematical underpinnings of AI and the rigorous systems engineering required to deploy it at scale. A Fellow of the Artificial Intelligence Board of America (ARTiBA), a three-time AI hackathon winner, and a Microsoft-certified AI Engineer, Ayo designs the distributed data platforms, large language models, and MLOps pipelines that transition AI from experimental phases into reliable production.

Under his leadership, TaxStreem is developing systems capable of comprehending tax legislation, maintaining accounting intelligence, and automating compliance through direct interaction with government platforms. Beyond his immediate role, Ayo’s long-term vision is to evolve AI into autonomous infrastructure that perceives, reasons, and acts within strict real-world constraints. Ultimately, he aims to build the technological foundations that will transition Africa from a consumer of global technology to a primary architect of its own intelligent economic infrastructure.
